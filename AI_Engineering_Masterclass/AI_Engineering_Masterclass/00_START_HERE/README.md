## Kaise Padhna Hai — Ek Senior Engineer Ki Advice

### Rule #1: Sequence Matter Karta Hai

Bhai, main jaanta hoon tum eager(excited) ho aur directly "Transformers" ya "Agents" padhna chahte ho. Mat karo yeh galti.

Har module pichle module ke upar build karta hai. Agar tum 01 ko skip karke 05 pe jump karo, toh tum woh engineer banoge jo **parrot kar sakta hai** but **samajh nahi sakta**. Interviews mein yahi log fail hote hain.

Sequence follow karo:
1. Pehle module padhho completely
2. Phir neeche ke "Interview Questions" dekho — kya tum genuinely answer kar sakte ho?
3. Agar nahi, toh file dobara padhho
4. Phir agla module

### Rule #2: Active Reading — Passive Reading Nahi

Har cheez padhte waqt khud se yeh questions karo:
- "Yeh concept kyun exist karta hai? Iske bina kya problem hogi?"
- "Yeh concept kaise doosre concepts se connected hai?"
- "Agar main yeh explain karna chahun ek 10-saal ke bachche ko, toh kya kahun?"

### Rule #3: The Feynman Technique

Richard Feynman (Nobel Prize winner physicist) ka ek simple method hai — agar tum koi cheez explain nahi kar sakte simple words mein, toh tum woh nahi jaante. 

Har topic ke baad ek blank page lo aur apne words mein likho jo samjha. Jahan ruko — wahan gap hai. Wahan dobara padhna hai.

### Rule #4: Duniya Se Connect Karo

Jab bhi tum koi concept padho, news mein, Twitter/X pe, LinkedIn pe — woh concept dhundho. Jab tum "ChatGPT ne yeh kiya" padhte ho, toh soch "Iska kaunsa module se connection hai?"

## Ek Baat Jo Main Chahta Hoon Tum Yaad Rakho

Jab main Anthropic mein tha, hum ek interesting pattern dekhte the interview process mein. Log jo sirf tools jaante the — "main LangChain use karta hoon, main PyTorch jaanta hoon" — woh consistently underperform karte the. 

Log jo **fundamentals deeply** jaante the — unhone consistently overperform kiya, chahe unhe specific tool ka experience na ho.

Kyunki AI field ek saal mein completely change ho jaata hai. Jo tools aaj popular hain, woh 2 saal mein outdated honge. Lekin jo fundamentals hain — math, architecture, principles — woh kabhi nahi change hote.

Yahi yeh course deta hai. **Fundamentals first, tools second.**

## Ek Story Sunao — Kyun Yeh Course Banaya

Imagine karo ek nayi recruitment. Ek bright developer hai — React, Node, databases — sab jaanta hai. Woh AI engineer banna chahta hai. Hum usse reject karte hain. Kyun? 

Interview mein jab hum poochte hain "Explain why attention mechanism works" — woh kehta hai "It's like a dictionary lookup." Theek hai, yeh ek analogy hai. Lekin jab hum deeper gaye — "Why does scaling up attention heads help? What exactly happens mathematically when you compute softmax over queries and keys?" — woh stuck ho jaata hai.

Doosri taraf ek candidate hai — kam experience, lekin concepts deep hain. Woh explain karta hai kaise attention weights form ek soft distribution over context, kaise different heads different relationships capture karte hain, kaise this enables parallelization unlike RNNs. Woh hire ho jaata hai.

**Woh depth is this course ka goal.**

AI Engineering Path | Machine Learning Deep — Ensemble Methods

Ensembles combine multiple models so the team beats any single member.

Bagging and boosting are the two ideas you will see most.

Key points:

- Bagging reduces variance by averaging diverse models
- Boosting reduces bias by fixing previous errors step by step
- Random Forests and gradient boosting are practical workhorses
- Ensembles can raise accuracy but also cost and complexity
- Production needs a clear win to justify ensemble overhead

Takeaway:
Ensemble power comes from diversity and disciplined combination, not from stacking blindly.

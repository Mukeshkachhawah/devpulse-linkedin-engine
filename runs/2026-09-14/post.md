AI Engineering Path | Mathematics for AI — Probability for ML

Models rarely output certainty. They output chances.

Probability helps you interpret scores, calibration, and risk.

Key points:

- A softmax score is a distribution over options, not absolute truth
- Bayes thinking reminds you that priors and evidence both matter
- Randomness in data and training means results need careful evaluation
- Confidence and correctness are not the same thing
- Good engineers communicate uncertainty instead of hiding it

Takeaway:
Probability turns “the model said so” into “the model estimates this, with limits.”

AI Engineering Path | Mathematics for AI — Optimization and Loss

A loss function defines what “wrong” means. Optimization is the search for lower wrongness.

Different losses create different model behavior.

Key points:

- Classification and regression usually need different losses
- Optimizer choice (SGD, Adam, and others) changes training dynamics
- Regularization trades pure training fit for better generalization
- Hyperparameters are part of the optimization design, not afterthoughts
- Diverging loss is a signal to stop and inspect before wasting compute

Takeaway:
Optimization is where math becomes a training plan you can debug.

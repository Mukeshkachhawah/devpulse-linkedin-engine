AI Engineering Path | History of AI — Early Groundwork

Before AI had a name, the groundwork was already forming.

Key points:

- World War II code-breaking showed that some hard problems need systematic machines, not only human speed
- In 1943, McCulloch and Pitts showed that brain-like units can be described with simple logic
- Their idea treated a neuron like a switch: inputs come in, a threshold is checked, an output goes out
- This made “artificial neurons” a real research idea decades before modern deep learning

Takeaway:
Modern AI looks new. Many core ideas are old. Strong engineering starts by knowing what was already proven, and what still failed.

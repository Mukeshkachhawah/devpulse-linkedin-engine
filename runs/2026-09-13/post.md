AI Engineering Path | Mathematics for AI — Gradients While Learning

During training, every weight gets a signal: increase me, decrease me, or stay.

That signal comes from gradients flowing back from the loss.

Key points:

- Large gradients can make updates jump too far
- Tiny gradients slow learning or stall deep networks
- Vanishing and exploding gradients are practical failure modes
- Activations, normalization, and residual links help gradient flow
- Good training curves are evidence that gradient signals are healthy

Takeaway:
If learning fails, ask what the gradients are doing before you blame the dataset alone.

AI Engineering Path | History of AI — The First Programs

After Dartmouth, researchers built early programs that looked impressive in small worlds.

Key points:

- Logic Theorist proved math theorems and even found shorter proofs for some of them
- General Problem Solver tried to solve many problems by breaking goals into smaller steps
- Arthur Samuel’s checkers program learned from its own play and helped popularize “machine learning”
- LISP became an early language for AI work because it handled symbols and lists well

These systems proved an important idea: problem solving can be treated as search. They also showed a hard limit: toy success does not automatically become real-world success.

Takeaway:
Early AI taught both hope and caution. Demos can open a field. Only scale, cost, and real use prove if the idea can last.

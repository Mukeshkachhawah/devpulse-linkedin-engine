AI Engineering Path | Mathematics for AI — Calculus and Gradients

Training a model means improving it a little at a time. Calculus tells us which direction is “a little better.”

A gradient points toward the steepest increase of a function. For loss, we usually step the opposite way.

Key points:

- Loss measures how wrong the model is
- Gradients tell each weight how to change to reduce loss
- Backpropagation is an efficient way to compute those gradients
- Learning rate controls how big each step is
- Unstable training often means gradients are too large or too small

Takeaway:
Gradient-based learning is not mystery. It is controlled improvement using derivatives.

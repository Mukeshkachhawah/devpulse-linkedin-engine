AI Engineering Path | Machine Learning Deep — Overfitting and Underfitting

Overfitting memorizes training data too well and fails on new data.
Underfitting is too simple and misses real patterns.

Both are failure modes. The goal is useful generalization.

Key points:

- Watch train vs validation gaps
- More complex models need stronger regularization and more data
- Early stopping, dropout, and simpler features can help
- Perfect training score is often a warning, not a victory
- Test on realistic slices of unseen data

Takeaway:
A model that only wins on yesterday’s examples is not ready for tomorrow’s users.

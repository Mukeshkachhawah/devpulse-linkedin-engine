AI Engineering Path | History of AI — Expert Systems Boom

Between the two AI winters, expert systems created a short boom.

These systems used rules written by human experts. One famous example helped configure complex computer orders and saved a lot of time and cost for a major company.

Key points:

- Businesses built AI teams around rule-based expert systems
- Special AI hardware and new AI companies grew quickly
- Japan also launched a large “intelligent computer” project
- For a short time, AI looked like a clear business win again

But rule systems had limits: knowledge was hard to write, hard to update, and weak outside their narrow area.

Takeaway:
A useful niche system can create a boom. It can also hide deep limits until the market asks for more.

AI Engineering Path | History of AI — The LLM Era

Large language models did not appear overnight. The public moment came later.

In 2017, the Transformer paper showed a new way to handle language: process tokens in parallel and use attention to relate words directly. That design scaled better than older sequence models.

Years of research followed. Then chat products made the work visible to everyone. Suddenly, AI was not only a research topic. It became a daily tool.

Key points:

- Transformers made large-scale language training more practical
- Pretraining on large text taught broad language patterns
- Chat interfaces made model power easy to try
- Useful products still need limits, evaluation, cost control, and safety checks

Takeaway:
For AI Engineering, the lesson is steady: strong foundations first, then products. Hype can spread fast. Reliable systems still need clear tests and real utility.

Next: Build with history in mind—judge every AI system by proof, cost, and limits.

AI Engineering Path | History of AI — Deep Learning Boom

Deep Learning became hard to ignore in 2012.

In the ImageNet contest, a neural network called AlexNet cut the error rate far below the previous best. It was not a small gain. It changed what people believed was possible.

Key points:

- Convolutional networks were not brand new, but scale made them powerful
- GPUs made large model training much faster
- Simpler activation functions helped deep networks train better
- Techniques like dropout helped reduce overfitting
- Better results in vision opened the door to bigger models in other areas

Takeaway:
Deep Learning showed that method, data, and hardware must move together. A good idea waits until compute and data can carry it.

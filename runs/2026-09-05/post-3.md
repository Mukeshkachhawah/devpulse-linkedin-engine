A 800-line pull request is not one review. It is several reviews stacked into one.

When a PR mixes a bug fix, a rename, and a new feature, reviewers cannot tell what to trust. They skim. Risk hides in the skim.

Prefer changes that answer one question:

- What is the smallest change that still delivers value?
- Can a reviewer understand it in one sitting?
- If something breaks, can you revert this without undoing unrelated work?

Small PRs are not about looking tidy. They are about making review honest and rollback safe.

Ship less at once. Understand more of what shipped.

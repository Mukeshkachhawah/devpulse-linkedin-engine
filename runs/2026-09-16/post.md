AI Engineering Path | Mathematics for AI — Entropy and Information

Entropy measures surprise. If outcomes are predictable, entropy is low. If anything can happen, entropy is high.

This idea appears in cross-entropy loss and many language model objectives.

Key points:

- Cross-entropy pushes predicted distributions toward true labels
- Low loss means the model is less “surprised” by the data
- Compression and prediction are deeply related ideas
- Attention and likelihood both deal with allocating probability mass
- Information thinking helps explain why good labels matter

Takeaway:
Training often means teaching the model to be less surprised by the right answers.

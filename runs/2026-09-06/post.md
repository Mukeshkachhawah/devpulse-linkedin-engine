AI Engineering Path | History of AI — Machine Learning Rises

After the AI winters, the field changed direction.

Old approach: write intelligence as rules from human experts.
New approach: let systems learn patterns from data.

Key points:

- Rules capture only part of real speech, images, and language
- Statistical methods improved speech recognition and other practical tasks
- Support Vector Machines and related tools beat older methods on many benchmarks
- Better data and clearer tests mattered as much as the algorithm
- Learning from examples scaled better than writing every rule by hand

Takeaway:
In AI Engineering, data quality and honest evaluation matter as much as model choice. Good systems start with good examples and clear checks.

AI Engineering Path | Mathematics for AI — Statistics That Matter

Statistics helps you decide if a result is real or just lucky.

In AI Engineering, this shows up in metrics, A/B tests, and evaluation design.

Key points:

- Mean and variance describe typical performance and spread
- A single lucky test set can hide weak generalization
- Sample size changes how much you should trust a metric
- Compare models with clear baselines, not vibes
- Track metric stability across slices, not only overall score

Takeaway:
A model is not “better” until the improvement survives honest measurement.

Most engineers treat ChatGPT like year zero of AI.

It is not.

AI did not start with deep learning in 2012 either. That was a loud chapter, not the first page.

A cleaner timeline for anyone building with this stuff:

- 1943: artificial neuron math
- 1950: Turing Test
- 1956: Dartmouth — the field gets its name
- 1970s–90s: winters when promises outran results
- 1980s–2000s: statistical ML ships spam filters, ranking, recommendations
- 2012: AlexNet makes deep learning impossible to ignore
- 2017+: Transformers, then the LLM product wave

Two myths that still waste interview answers and product bets:

1. Transformers were invented by OpenAI.
   No. Google Brain published Attention Is All You Need in 2017. OpenAI scaled and productized.

2. ChatGPT was the first chatbot.
   No. ELIZA was 1966. ChatGPT won on capability and distribution, not originality of the format.

History is useful here for one practical reason.

It trains you to separate a breakthrough from a reset button — so the next hype cycle does not erase what already worked.

AI Engineering Path | Machine Learning Deep — Training vs Inference

Training and inference are different jobs.

Training is when the model learns. Inference is when the trained model answers.

Key points:

- Training needs more compute, data pipelines, and experiment tracking
- Inference needs latency, cost, reliability, and stable outputs
- A great training metric can still fail in production inference
- Batch training and real-time serving have different constraints
- Engineers must design for both stages, not only model accuracy

Takeaway:
Build systems for the full lifecycle. Learning is only half the product.

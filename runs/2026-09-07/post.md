AI Engineering Path | History of AI — Deep Learning Boom

Today I studied the 2012 ImageNet moment — the year Deep Learning stopped being a niche idea.

AlexNet did not win by a small margin. It cut the error rate far below the previous best. That result changed what engineers believed was practical.

What stuck with me as a software engineer:

- CNNs already existed; scale and GPUs made them useful in production-size problems
- Better hardware was not a side detail — it unlocked the method
- Small training details (activations, dropout) mattered as much as the architecture
- A clear win in vision gave teams confidence to try deeper models elsewhere

My takeaway for building systems:
A good method is not enough. Data, compute, and engineering have to move together. Otherwise the idea stays stuck in a paper.

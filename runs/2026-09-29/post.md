AI Engineering Path | Deep Learning Core — Depth and Nonlinearity

A stack of only linear layers collapses into one linear map.

Nonlinearity is what makes depth powerful.

Key points:

- Activations like ReLU create useful nonlinear decision boundaries
- Depth composes features: edges to shapes to objects, sounds to phonemes
- Too little nonlinearity underfits complex structure
- Too unstable nonlinearity or depth can hurt training
- Architecture choices are tradeoffs among capacity, stability, and cost

Takeaway:
Depth without nonlinearity is mostly expensive linear algebra. Together, they unlock representation power.
